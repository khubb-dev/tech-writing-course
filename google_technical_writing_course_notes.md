# Google Technical Writing Course Notes 
## Technical Writing One 

* If a new or unfamiliar term already exists, link it to an existing explanation.
* Define new terms if the document introduces it.
* Collect the definitions into a glossary if multiple new terms are introduced.
* Use terms consistently.
* Use acronyms when the acronym is significantly shorter than the full term.
* Use acronyms when it appears many times throughout the document.
* Do not define acronyms that will only be used a few times. 
* Convert several facts separated by commas into a bulleted list.
* Repeat the noun instead of using the pronoun when more than five words separate the noun from the pronoun.
* Pronouns which cause the most confusion include: it, they, them, their, this, that.
* Place a noun immediately after **this** or **that** to avoid confusion.
* Use **active voice** instead of passive voice. 
* Be bold -- be active.
* passive verb = form of be + past participle verb

* Keep sentences under *25 words* whenever possible.
